Wordmarks and lockups for homie. The wordmark is Fredoka 600 converted to outlines, so the files need no font.

- `homie-wordmark-he.svg`, `homie-wordmark-en.svg`: black, for light backgrounds.
- `homie-wordmark-he-white.svg`, `homie-wordmark-en-white.svg`: white, for `ink` and `accent` backgrounds.
- `homie-lockup-he.svg`, `homie-lockup-en.svg`: wordmark with the app icon, for headers and sharing images.

Hebrew is the primary version. Do not recolor, stretch or retype.
