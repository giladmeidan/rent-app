# Standing product decisions (read before writing any spec)

Updated: 2026-11-13. Full reasoning in the Product Plan doc.

| Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Whole-apartment rentals AND both roommate flows (room available / looking to join) from MVP |
| Language | Hebrew only at launch; i18n foundations for other languages from the first commit; RTL base layout |
| Brokers | Not allowed at launch. Owner cap: 3 active listings per account (admin may raise). Broker account type reserved in the data model for a later paid tier |
| Platform | Web first, responsive, PWA-installable |
| Brand | Decided 2026-11-12 (Gilad): the product is **homie / הומי**; the brand kit `design/brand/` (tokens.json, logos, mascot, icons, voice) wins over `design/screens-v1/` for colour, type (Fredoka + Rubik), shape, voice and name; the artboards keep layout and anatomy only. Voice: never one gender and no slash forms (plural, infinitive or noun). **No new artboards**: screens without one take their design in the spec, from the kit and the existing artboards (see `specs/0009-brand-amendment.md`) |
| Slice 9 copy + UX | Decided 2026-11-12 (Gilad, all recommendations of 0009a/b/f/k/o): landing claims "מאומתים באימייל", "מגישים מועמדות בהודעה קצרה", "חינם למחפשים"; CTAs "פרסום דירה" / "חיפוש דירה"; no footer until Slice 12; a signed-in user at `/` goes to their home; no header "הרשמה" button; Fredoka + Rubik self-hosted; search counts say "מודעות"; owner inbox statuses in the feminine (מועמדות); chat "מתי נוח לכם?" / "בשליחה…"; "מחפשים חוזה של {months}"; missing flatmate ages show "?"; parking chips "חניה חלקית" / "חניה לא נדרשת"; a link out of every join-flat state; a flatmate empty-chat line; rooms stay an exact-number select; no back link in the new-listing wizard; my-listings cards show the expiry date. Counts use i18next plural forms. The orchestrator merges Slice 9 + 9b PRs after green CI and local e2e. Offline (0009f, Gilad): requests fail fast (`networkMode: 'always'`) and show "אין חיבור לאינטרנט"; an action started offline is not replayed on reconnect, the user retries. Map pins (0009t, Gilad): overlapping pins stay at their true positions under WCAG 2.5.8's essential exception; axe excludes only `.maplibregl-marker`; clustering is a follow-up |
| Pricing | Free; free-tier infrastructure. Paid services need an explicit decision from Gilad |
| Launch area | Gush Dan only; areas are a config table, listings outside are hidden |
| Gender preference | Hard filter on ROOM listings and room-seeker profiles only; never on whole-apartment listings. Slice 7: a misfit room is **hidden** from that seeker, see "Rooms" |
| Rooms | Decided 2026-11-11 (Slice 7 batch, Gilad): a room listing adds who it is for (נשים / גברים / לא משנה, default לא משנה; an apartment is always לא משנה), how many flatmates live there and, optionally, their ages; existing room listings get לא משנה and become public with Slice 7. Search showed **apartments and rooms together** by default, with an apartment/room type filter, until Slice 10: since then rooms live in the market's own search (`/market`) and the apartment search shows apartments only (see "Search" and "שוק השותפים"). A seeker fills the profile's **room section** only if looking for a room (Slice 10, 0010c: gender, birth year and the answers move to the profile's personal part, which any account fills; the flatmates preference moves to the "what I am looking for" part): own gender (אישה / גבר / אחר), an optional birth year (the one exception to "Matching"'s no-ages rule), an optional flatmates preference (נשים / גברים / לא משנה) and the questionnaire. A room whose preference does not fit the seeker's gender (women-only for a man or "אחר", men-only for a woman or "אחר"), or that contradicts the seeker's flatmates preference (men-only for "נשים", women-only for "גברים"), is **hidden from that seeker's results** (unlike other hard filters, never capped and shown) and applying to it is refused; a לא משנה room is never hidden by the seeker's preference; a seeker without a room section sees every room and must fill it before applying to a women- or men-only room. Ages are optional on both sides and drive only the "שותפים בגילך" chip. The **questionnaire** is 7 fixed questions on a 3-point scale (cleanliness, guests, quiet hours, social, kosher kitchen, Shabbat, working from home), optional with a nudge, answered by the seeker and by the room's side; it is a soft criterion on room listings only (weight 20; it can raise or lower the score, never filters; orchestrator 2026-11-11 on 0007g's recommendation, under Gilad's Slice 7 delegation). On a **room** application the owner's inbox shows the applicant's gender, age, score and reasons, with the questionnaire answers (and later details) behind a "see more"; never on apartment applications. Room listers keep the owner role (no role switch in Slice 7); since Slice 10 any account posts a room (no roles in the market, see "Account roles"). Group chats with flatmates: Slice 8. **Room privacy** (Gilad 2026-11-11, 0007b rule 9, option (b)): a room's flatmate ages and questionnaire answers are returned on the public listing responses (search, map, listing page) only to a signed-in seeker; anonymous visitors and owners get none there (an owner still sees their own in "my listings"); revisit in the pre-launch privacy pass. **Who is a seeker** for this rule since Slice 10 (Gilad 2026-11-13, decision 13): an account with the seeker role **or** any account whose profile has the "what I am looking for" part filled; flatmates shown on a room's page (0010f) follow the same rule |
| People & likes | Decided 2026-11-11 (Slice 8 batch, Gilad): **people browse is for room listers only**: an owner with a live room listing browses seekers who have a room section and switched on "show me to flats looking for a flatmate" (opt-in, default off); apartment owners and seekers do not browse people. A person card shows the display name, age (if given), gender, preferred areas, move-in, the questionnaire answers, an optional "about me" (up to 300 characters) and the match score for the lister's room; the budget only as fits / does not fit, **never the amount**. **Mutual interest is two-way likes**: a lister likes a seeker for one of their rooms, a seeker likes a room; **both sides see the likes they received** ("liked you"), and liking back makes it mutual; no chat until both liked. A mutual like **becomes a shortlisted application** tagged "mutual match" in the room's applicant inbox and in the seeker's "my applications", so chat, un-shortlist, decline and withdraw work as in Slices 4–6. **Widened in Slice 10** (Gilad 2026-11-13, see "שוק השותפים"; the room-side flow above stays): people browse people; the entry ticket to browse is your own visible card or a live room, but **liking a person needs your own visible card** (a lister with a live room and no card browses and gets 403 `card_required` on a like, with a nudge to show the card); a mutual person-to-person like opens a **direct chat** with no application; either side can remove the match, after which the chat stays readable and sending closes, the two reappear in each other's browse, and a new mutual like reopens the same chat; a market person card shows no score, only the questionnaire compared with the viewer's answers |
| Flatmates | Decided 2026-11-11 (Slice 8 batch, Gilad): a room's lister shares an **invite link**; a signed-in user who opens it joins that room's flat; every chat about the room (applications and mutual matches) then includes the lister, the flatmates and the candidate; flatmates **chat only** (shortlist and decline stay with the lister); a flatmate sees messages **from the moment they joined**, and the chat header lists every member; decided 2026-11-11 (0008d build, Gilad): a seeker whose application to the room was **declined cannot join** its flat (409 `declined`), because the link can reach them from a flatmate, not only the owner; a seeker with an open application withdraws before joining |
| שוק השותפים | Decided 2026-11-13 (Gilad): Slice 10 and later are pushed back; the roommate side becomes its own area of the app, **"שוק השותפים"**, a main feature. Room listings leave the shared search and live in the market, apart from apartments; the existing room logic stays (questionnaire, gender rule, people browse + likes, flatmates invite, group chat). Inside the market there are **no owner/seeker roles**: any account can post a room or be a person looking. **People browse people**: people looking see each other, not only room listers; this is the base for a later person-to-person match rating. A mutual like between two people **opens a direct chat** (no room, no application). You browse people only if your own card is visible, through **one visibility switch** ("show my card in שוק השותפים") that covers flats and other people. The **gender rule works both ways**: a person is hidden from anyone whose gender contradicts their flatmates preference. **Photos** are built with the market, for rooms, one per person, and apartments at the same time; **Cloudflare R2 is approved** for them (free tier), and photos are served from random, unguessable public addresses that never contain a user or listing id (Gilad: important). An edit **can no longer switch** a listing between apartment and room (replaces "Listing edit"). Flatmate ages and answers on a room stay visible **only to seekers**, not to every signed-in user. The profile **splits in two**: a personal part (gender, age, about me, answers, photo) anyone can fill alone, and a "what I am looking for" part needed only to apply or to appear as a person looking. A flatmate is shown on the room's page **only by their own choice** at join, default off. A **report button** is not part of the market slice; it comes in the later trust slices and is certain to be added. Accepted the same day (all of `specs/MARKET-PLAN.md` section 6): **navigation** is a דירות / שוק השותפים switch at the top, the bottom bar shows the chosen side's own tabs, chat is in both; a room **keeps its address** `/listings/:id`, and old search links filtered to rooms redirect to the market search; the **cap of 3 live listings is shared** by apartments and rooms; the page a signed-in person lands on is unchanged, and the landing page gains one entry point to the market; "a person looking" is the **existing profile, open to every account**; signup from the market skips the owner/seeker question and stores seeker; **photo limits**: up to 10 per listing, 8 MB per upload, none required to publish, the first is the cover, one per person (both stored sizes of a person photo, 480 px and 1600 px, are reachable by the same people who may see the small one; Gilad 2026-11-13, 0010j); a **live room or a visible card** both count as the entry ticket to people browse; either side can **remove a person-to-person match**, after which the chat stays readable and sending closes; a person card shows **no score** until the rating exists, only the questionnaire compared with the viewer's own answers; joined flatmates who chose to be shown appear as people, the typed count stays and typed ages remain for the others; the **lister answers the flat's questionnaire**, and each shown flatmate's own answers appear on their card; size and furnished stay the flat's, and the new room facts (room size, private bathroom, balcony, bills, who is posting) are **all optional**; the lister is **not counted** in "flatmates living there" and appears as a person only if they say they live there; the market's specs are `0010*`. Findings and build order: `specs/MARKET-PLAN.md` **Decided 2026-11-14 (Gilad, Slice 10 second half, the recommended option each):** (Q1) on a women- or men-only room, a viewer who has not set a gender sees **no people** on the room's page (flatmates and lister), and a declared gender must fit the room's preference; (Q2) a shown person without a "what I am looking for" part gets **no own gender-visibility setting** for now: the room's gender preference protects them; (Q3) the profile-photo hint **lists every place** the photo shows (applications, the market, a room's page, people browse, chat) and is updated whenever a place is added |
| Applicant count | Shown to seekers as the exact number; Slice 4 logs applications-per-view to revisit |
| Account roles | Every account picks **owner** or **seeker** at signup (switchable later via a profile edit, slice TBD). Broker is an `account_type`, not a role (decided 2026-11-09). **Inside שוק השותפים roles do not apply** (Slice 10, Gilad 2026-11-13): any account posts and manages rooms, applies to and likes rooms, and has a profile; signup started from the market skips the owner/seeker question and stores seeker. Apartments keep the roles: only owners post apartments, only seekers apply to them |
| Signup data | Email + display name (2–40 chars) only. No phone number is collected until SMS verification is switched on (decided 2026-11-09) |
| OTP | Email OTP in Slice 1; SMS adapter stubbed behind an interface; switched on at launch readiness |
| Matching | Hard filters (budget overlap, move-in window ±4 weeks, shared area, deal-breakers) then weighted distance score, 0–100, with top-3 agreeing / top-1 differing explanation. Decided 2026-11-11 (Slice 6 batch, Gilad): a listing that fails a hard filter **stays in results**, its score capped at 59 and the failed filter shown as its "differs" line (as `SearchDesktop.dc.html`); the seeker profile holds **housing preferences only** (max budget, move-in date + flexible, preferred areas, household single/couple/family/roommates, pets, smoker, contract length, parking needed; no ages, occupation or income); owners get a wizard step "העדפות" (accepted households, smoking allowed, minimum contract length) where **smoking and pets are deal-breakers** and household and contract length only lower the score; **a seeker must have a profile to apply** (applicants from before Slice 6 show no score). Decided 2026-11-11 (0006c security finding, Gilad chose (a)): the owner's inbox match scores budget and area against the listing's **price and area at apply time** (snapshot on the application), so an owner cannot recover a seeker's budget or areas by editing the listing and re-reading the score; the seeker's own view stays live, so the two sides differ only after the owner edits price or area |
| Chat | Opens only on owner shortlist or mutual interest; never one-sided. Decided 2026-11-11 (Slice 5 batch): new messages are **pushed** to open pages (server-sent events over Redis pub/sub, one stream per tab, `0005c`; Gilad asked for the option that holds at a very large user count, the orchestrator chose SSE over polling and WebSockets); after an un-shortlist, decline or withdraw the thread stays readable by both and sending closes, a re-shortlist reopens it; no chat notifications in Slice 5 (header count only; unread messages join the pre-launch daily digest email); one conversation per application, with the application's id (Slice 10's 0010g gives a conversation its own id, so a direct person-to-person chat has no application); "הצעת סיור" is a composer chip that inserts editable template text |
| Trust (MVP) | Phone/email-verified accounts, report button everywhere, rate limits, listing auto-expiry **3 months (90 days from creation)** (changed from 30 days, Gilad 2026-11-10), deposit-before-viewing warning in chat |
| Listings | Decided 2026-11-10 (0002a batch): a listing has the CreateListing/Listing artboards' facts, no photos until Slice 10 (`0010d` / `0010e`: up to 10 per listing, none required, the first is the cover); **no drafts** (live on publish, the wizard's preview is the review step); a room listing adds the Slice 7 room fields and is public since 0007b; arnona is entered as the bi-monthly bill, vaad monthly; the 3-listing cap counts **live** listings only (paused or expired free a slot; a total-rows cap per owner is a later follow-up) |
| Listing edit | Decided 2026-11-10 (0002e batch): owners edit a listing's fields from "my listings"; **an expired listing is not editable** (no edit link, the edit page refuses it) until the listing renewal (now with trust, Slice 11); unsaved edits are guarded on reload/close and the page's own back link; **an edit can no longer switch apartment/room** (Slice 10, Gilad 2026-11-13, decision 12; until then it could) |
| Applications | Decided 2026-11-10 (Slice 4 batch, all six accepted): no notifications in Slice 4 (owner sees a new-applicant badge, seeker sees the status; a daily digest email to owners is its own small spec before launch); re-apply allowed after withdrawing, never after a decline (Gilad 2026-11-11: a later slice adds one more chance after a decline, rule TBD — e.g. once, after a month; see FOLLOWUPS); the owner's identity is not shown to applicants until chat opens on shortlist (Slice 5; one exception since Slice 10: a room lister who says they live there is shown on the room page, to seekers only, see "Search"); the application message is required, 1–500 characters; a declined seeker sees the true status in soft wording ("לא נבחרה"); a decline is final, behind a confirm dialog; `0004c` Q-A/Q-B (2026-11-10): a re-apply counts as new and lands on top of the owner's newest-first inbox; owners see applicant counts only, never a view count (views stay recorded for the applications-per-view log) |
| Search | Decided 2026-11-10 (0003a batch, all 7 accepted): search and the listing page are **public** (no login to browse; applying needs login in Slice 4); list first, the map is `0003c`; map tiles: **public OSM tiles while traffic is tiny, self-hosted Protomaps on R2 once R2 is approved**; default sort newest, plus price up/down, until Slice 6's match score; entry-date filter = "available by my move-in date", a flexible (±2 weeks) listing counts 14 days earlier; the public listing shows **no owner name** and the street **without a house number**; room listings are public since Slice 7 (0007b), next to apartments. **Since Slice 10** (Gilad 2026-11-13) rooms leave this search and live in the market's own public search `/market`; the apartment search and its map show apartments only (`GET /listings` without `listing_type` means apartments) and old `/search?listing_type=room` links redirect to `/market`. **Exception to "no owner name"** (Gilad 2026-11-13, 0010f option (a)): when a room's lister says they live there (who is posting = "גרים בדירה"), the room page shows the lister's full card (name, photo, age, gender, about me, answers) to seekers only, and the wizard says so when they pick it |
| Map | Decided 2026-11-13 (Gilad, map research in `specs/map-research/`): the full map view stays a feature of its own; bundle 1 (pins that say more) and bundle 2 (the map as part of search) go ahead as specs `0009v`–`0009y` (Slice 9c); bundle 3 (Protomaps vector style, neighbourhoods, transit, commute) waits until they ship. Decisions: (1) a pin shows the match score **only for high matches (80+)**, other chips show the price alone, and the chip colour carries the tier; (2) "seen" is **per device** (browser storage, cleared on logout), per account is not planned; (3) a **side map** beside the list from 1280 px **mirrors the list** (its pins are the loaded listings, panning never filters the list) with a button that opens the full map at the same place; (4) the map position and selected pin are remembered **in the URL**; (5) the side map ships on **public OSM tiles**, which stay until launch; the Protomaps switch keeps its place before going public and is not moved earlier; (6) the listing page circle shows an **honest radius of about 300 m**; (7) on the side map the **cards are the keyboard path**, its pins are not tab stops. Clustering (0009v) ends 0009t's WCAG 2.5.8 "essential" exemption for map pins |
| Stack | React+TS+Vite+Tailwind, FastAPI, PostgreSQL+PostGIS, Redis, arq worker, MapLibre+OSM tiles, Nominatim, Cloudflare R2/Pages, Fly.io or Railway |
