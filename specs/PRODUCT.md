| Rooms | Decided 2026-11-11 (Slice 7 batch, Gilad): a room listing adds who it is for (נשים / גברים / לא משנה, default לא משנה; an apartment is always לא משנה), how many flatmates live there and, optionally, their ages; existing room listings get לא משנה and become public with Slice 7. Search showed **apartments and rooms together** by default, with an apartment/room type filter, until Slice 10: since then rooms live in the market's own search (`/market`) and the apartment search shows apartments only (see "Search" and "שוק השותפים"). A seeker fills the profile's **room section** only if looking for a room (Slice 10, 0010c: gender, birth year and the answers move to the profile's personal part, which any account fills; the flatmates preference moves to the "what I am looking for" part): own gender (אישה / גבר / אחר), an optional birth year (the one exception to "Matching"'s no-ages rule), an optional flatmates preference (נשים / גברים / לא משנה) and the questionnaire. A room whose preference does not fit the seeker's gender (women-only for a man or "אחר", men-only for a woman or "אחר"), or that contradicts the seeker's flatmates preference (men-only for "נשים", women-only for "גברים"), is **hidden from that seeker's results** (unlike other hard filters, never capped and shown) and applying to it is refused; a לא משנה room is never hidden by the seeker's preference; a seeker without a room section sees every room and must fill it before applying to a women- or men-only room. Ages are optional on both sides and drive only the "שותפים בגילך" chip. The **questionnaire** is 7 fixed questions on a 3-point scale (cleanliness, guests, quiet hours, social, kosher kitchen, Shabbat, working from home), optional with a nudge, answered by the seeker and by the room's side; it is a soft criterion on room listings only (weight 20; it can raise or lower the score, never filters; orchestrator 2026-11-11 on 0007g's recommendation, under Gilad's Slice 7 delegation). On a **room** application the owner's inbox shows the applicant's gender, age, score and reasons, with the questionnaire answers (and later details) behind a "see more"; never on apartment applications. Room listers keep the owner role (no role switch in Slice 7); since Slice 10 any account posts a room (no roles in the market, see "Account roles"). Group chats with flatmates: Slice 8. **Room privacy** (Gilad 2026-11-11, 0007b rule 9, option (b)): a room's flatmate ages and questionnaire answers are returned on the public listing responses (search, map, listing page) only to a signed-in seeker; anonymous visitors and owners get none there (an owner still sees their own in "my listings"); revisit in the pre-launch privacy pass. **Who is a seeker** for this rule since Slice 10 (Gilad 2026-11-13, decision 13): an account with the seeker role **or** any account whose profile has the "what I am looking for" part filled; flatmates shown on a room's page (0010f) follow the same rule |
| People & likes | Decided 2026-11-11 (Slice 8 batch, Gilad): **people browse is for room listers only**: an owner with a live room listing browses seekers who have a room section and switched on "show me to flats looking for a flatmate" (opt-in, default off); apartment owners and seekers do not browse people. A person card shows the display name, age (if given), gender, preferred areas, move-in, the questionnaire answers, an optional "about me" (up to 300 characters) and the match score for the lister's room; the budget only as fits / does not fit, **never the amount**. **Mutual interest is two-way likes**: a lister likes a seeker for one of their rooms, a seeker likes a room; **both sides see the likes they received** ("liked you"), and liking back makes it mutual; no chat until both liked. A mutual like **becomes a shortlisted application** tagged "mutual match" in the room's applicant inbox and in the seeker's "my applications", so chat, un-shortlist, decline and withdraw work as in Slices 4–6. **Widened in Slice 10** (Gilad 2026-11-13, see "שוק השותפים"; the room-side flow above stays): people browse people; the entry ticket to browse is your own visible card or a live room, but **liking a person needs your own visible card** (a lister with a live room and no card browses and gets 403 `card_required` on a like, with a nudge to show the card); a mutual person-to-person like opens a **direct chat** with no application; either side can remove the match, after which the chat stays readable and sending closes, the two reappear in each other's browse, and a new mutual like reopens the same chat; a market person card shows no score, only the questionnaire compared with the viewer's answers. **Changed 2026-11-14 (Gilad, spec 0010l):** on a room a seeker has **one** action, the application; the seeker's room-like is removed. A lister's like of a person in people browse stays and becomes an **invite**: when that seeker applies, the application is shortlisted at once (tagged "mutual match") and the chat opens. The lister's inbox is the one place for people interested in a room; people browse shows opted-in people only. Person-to-person likes in the market are unchanged. **Changed 2026-11-14 (Gilad, spec 0010m): two streams.** (1) **Likes are only person ↔ person, to search together**: seeing people in the market (`/market/people`, the likes page) needs your **own visible card**; a live room is **no longer** an entry ticket (replaces "your own visible card or a live room" above and in "שוק השותפים"). (2) **Room ↔ seeker is application + invite**, never called a like: the seeker applies, the lister invites from the room's own people page (no card needed); a seeker's invites show in the market's "my activity" next to their room applications, not on the likes page, and the invite count sits on that tab. The invite stays its own record (not an application row) |
| Flatmates | Decided 2026-11-11 (Slice 8 batch, Gilad): a room's lister shares an **invite link**; a signed-in user who opens it joins that room's flat; every chat about the room (applications and mutual matches) then includes the lister, the flatmates and the candidate; flatmates **chat only** (shortlist and decline stay with the lister); a flatmate sees messages **from the moment they joined**, and the chat header lists every member; decided 2026-11-11 (0008d build, Gilad): a seeker whose application to the room was **declined cannot join** its flat (409 `declined`), because the link can reach them from a flatmate, not only the owner; a seeker with an open application withdraws before joining |
| שוק השותפים | Decided 2026-11-13 (Gilad): Slice 10 and later are pushed back; the roommate side becomes its own area of the app, **"שוק השותפים"**, a main feature. Room listings leave the shared search and live in the market, apart from apartments; the existing room logic stays (questionnaire, gender rule, people browse + likes, flatmates invite, group chat). Inside the market there are **no owner/seeker roles**: any account can post a room or be a person looking. **People browse people**: people looking see each other, not only room listers; this is the base for a later person-to-person match rating. A mutual like between two people **opens a direct chat** (no room, no application). You browse people only if your own card is visible, through **one visibility switch** ("show my card in שוק השותפים") that covers flats and other people. The **gender rule works both ways**: a person is hidden from anyone whose gender contradicts their flatmates preference. **Photos** are built with the market, for rooms, one per person, and apartments at the same time; **Cloudflare R2 is approved** for them (free tier), and photos are served from random, unguessable public addresses that never contain a user or listing id (Gilad: important). An edit **can no longer switch** a listing between apartment and room (replaces "Listing edit"). Flatmate ages and answers on a room stay visible **only to seekers**, not to every signed-in user. The profile **splits in two**: a personal part (gender, age, about me, answers, photo) anyone can fill alone, and a "what I am looking for" part needed only to apply or to appear as a person looking. A flatmate is shown on the room's page **only by their own choice** at join, default off. A **report button** is not part of the market slice; it comes in the later trust slices and is certain to be added. Accepted the same day (all of `specs/archive/MARKET-PLAN.md` section 6): **navigation** is a דירות / שוק השותפים switch at the top, the bottom bar shows the chosen side's own tabs, chat is in both; a room **keeps its address** `/listings/:id`, and old search links filtered to rooms redirect to the market search; the **cap of 3 live listings is shared** by apartments and rooms; the page a signed-in person lands on is unchanged, and the landing page gains one entry point to the market; "a person looking" is the **existing profile, open to every account**; signup from the market skips the owner/seeker question and stores seeker; **photo limits**: up to 10 per listing, 8 MB per upload, none required to publish, the first is the cover, one per person (both stored sizes of a person photo, 480 px and 1600 px, are reachable by the same people who may see the small one; Gilad 2026-11-13, 0010j); a **live room or a visible card** both count as the entry ticket to people browse (**changed 2026-11-14, spec 0010m: the visible card only**, see "People & likes"); either side can **remove a person-to-person match**, after which the chat stays readable and sending closes; a person card shows **no score** until the rating exists, only the questionnaire compared with the viewer's own answers; joined flatmates who chose to be shown appear as people, the typed count stays and typed ages remain for the others; the **lister answers the flat's questionnaire**, and each shown flatmate's own answers appear on their card; size and furnished stay the flat's, and the new room facts (room size, private bathroom, balcony, bills, who is posting) are **all optional**; the lister is **not counted** in "flatmates living there" and appears as a person only if they say they live there; the market's specs are `0010*`. Findings and build order: `specs/archive/MARKET-PLAN.md`. **Decided 2026-11-14 (Gilad, Slice 10 second half, the recommended option each):** (Q1) on a women- or men-only room, a viewer who has not set a gender sees **no people** on the room's page (flatmates and lister), and a declared gender must fit the room's preference; (Q2) a shown person without a "what I am looking for" part gets **no own gender-visibility setting** for now: the room's gender preference protects them; (Q3) the profile-photo hint **lists every place** the photo shows (applications, the market, a room's page, people browse, chat) and is updated whenever a place is added |
